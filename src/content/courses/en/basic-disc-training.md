## About the course

Basic training and certification is the first step to using the PulsAnalys tools. The course covers DISC theory and how to give feedback on a profile. We go through the background and interpretation of different profiles, with examples of where PulsAnalys fits.

The main aim of the first two days is for you to understand DISC theory and be able to run and give feedback on Personal Profiles. The certification day takes place about three to four weeks after the first two days, on a date the participants agree together. The course includes ten profiles to practise on before certification. Throughout, we follow our purpose-built feedback structure and our thorough ethical guidelines.

## The system

During the course you learn every function in PulsAnalys: creating profiles and links, producing reports, creating team and recruitment profiles and running 360 profiles. You have full access to our web-based system and all our products throughout the course.

Before the course we send you a link to complete your own Personal Profile, which you use for practice during the course.
