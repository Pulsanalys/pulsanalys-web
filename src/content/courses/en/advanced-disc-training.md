## About the course

This advanced DISC course teaches you more about our Team Profile, Recruitment Profile and 360 Profile. It is a full day and gives you the knowledge and skills to work with all PulsAnalys DISC products – and to use PulsAnalys as a tool in your organisation.

The course is for managers, leaders, HR professionals, coaches, consultants and recruiters. It has three parts.

### Team profiles

We create links and team profiles and show the steps for building a requirements profile for a team. We discuss the process behind the results, and you get ideas for exercises you can use when working with groups.

### Recruitment profiles

We create links and recruitment profiles and work through the steps for building a requirements profile. We also discuss the process behind the results.

### 360 profiles

We broaden and deepen your knowledge of the Personal Profile and the 360 Profile, and you learn to use both in different kinds of development work.
