## About the training

This training is for people who lead others. Its main aim is awareness of your own strengths and your team's – and through that, more engagement, motivation, effectiveness and profitability.

You start by completing your own Personal Profile to see where your strengths lie and how to use them even better. We then coach you on using your strengths in your leadership, and on the pitfalls to watch for.

The training runs over eight sessions of about 60 minutes, mainly online. You get access to the material and can watch the sessions afterwards if you want to recap or missed one. The training ends with a diploma.
