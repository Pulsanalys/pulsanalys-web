## What is a 360 Profile?

The 360 Profile measures how others perceive a person's behaviour. It is a DISC analysis in which people rate each other and give feedback. It is used to develop collaboration between managers and employees, and between colleagues.

The analysis is a very effective tool for improving communication, feedback and collaboration at work, and gives a full picture of how a person operates in different situations and contexts.

The 360 Profile and the Personal Profile have a lot in common. The difference is that instead of rating yourself, you rate a colleague – and so give feedback on how you see them.

## Where it is used

The 360 Profile is used effectively in leadership, communication, group and team development, performance reviews and reference checks in recruitment.
