## What is a Team Profile?

The Team Profile is used to build high-performing teams. It gives you a clear picture of the people in a working group and a starting point for team building and team development.

The tool shows the team's strengths clearly, alongside the requirements profile the team has agreed together. The requirements profile is then matched against each individual's Personal Profile.

The Team Profile highlights the qualities the team considers important. It supports the team leader's leadership, communication and feedback, and gives guidance on how to reach the team's goals.

## Where it is used

The Team Profile is used effectively in leadership, group and team development, sales development and communication.
