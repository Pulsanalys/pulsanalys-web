## What is a Personal Profile?

The Personal Profile measures a person's behaviour. The strength of this DISC analysis lies in how precisely it shows how a person prioritises in different situations. This reveals the roles in which the person is most comfortable – and where they are likely to fit and thrive.

Our DISC analysis measures three things: where we are at our most comfortable – our social self –, how we adapt in a work situation and how we adapt under pressure.

The Personal Profile can be used for personal development, performance reviews and coaching, among other things. It is also the basis for the other DISC analyses that PulsAnalys AB offers.

## Where it is used

The Personal Profile is used effectively in personal development, leadership, communication, group and team development, the psychosocial work environment, sales development and performance reviews.

We run regular courses where you learn to use the tool. If you prefer, our consultants can create Personal Profiles for you and your organisation.
