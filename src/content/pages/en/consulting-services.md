## Tailored lectures

We regularly give inspirational lectures around the country, and we can design lectures specifically for your organisation. [Read more about our lectures](/en/webinars/).

## Personal and staff development

Do you want to develop your communication, or simply gain new insight into yourself? We design training for your workplace focused on personal and staff development. Communication is the hardest thing there is – so how do we understand each other better at work? PulsAnalys DISC increases understanding and awareness in the workplace and supports diversity, so you benefit from every employee's strengths.

## Management and leadership development

Good leadership needs constant maintenance and development. A workplace is dynamic, and a good leader has to keep up. PulsAnalys DISC helps you stay on top of what is happening in your workplace and what drives and motivates your people. Better understanding of this is a sound basis for a working climate where everyone thrives and performs.

## Recruitment

Recruiting the wrong person can be costly. The PulsAnalys DISC Personal Profile is nuanced and complete, and gives you a very good picture of your candidates' soft skills. What drives and motivates the person? How do they relate to the rest of the team? Which qualities can you as an employer benefit from? We give you the full picture of your recruitment.

## Team and group development

Team days are one of our most popular consulting services. PulsAnalys DISC is an excellent tool for capturing and directing your team's interests and drivers. How can your team develop further and work together even better? We go through the profiles in the team and how you communicate and interact in the best possible climate. Differences are a strength and can be used positively when you have the tools for it.

## Conflict management

Conflict at work can be hard to get to grips with. What triggered it? Who is involved? How do we build understanding and untie the knots? PulsAnalys DISC is a communication tool that brings both the individual's and the group's interests into focus. Resolving a conflict takes sensitivity and sound leadership. We help you improve wellbeing and the atmosphere in your workplace.
