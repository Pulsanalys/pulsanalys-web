PulsAnalys AB was founded in the mid-1990s by Robert Mellin. It is a Swedish company that also works internationally, and is today owned and run by Mia Wernersson. PulsAnalys AB is represented in several places in Sweden, and we are looking for partners in the Nordic countries, Italy and Spain.

PulsAnalys AB provides analysis and measurement tools for long-term development and change in leadership, organisation and human behaviour. We work with customers in both the private and the public sector, and with psychologists, behavioural scientists and business developers with long experience of development and change. Our people have many years of leadership experience across different industries.

We work with long-term, sustainable personal development, which we see as the key to success. That is why we offer several analysis tools, used according to the need.

## Our vision

To increase understanding between employees and managers with simple tools – raising motivation and effectiveness, which leads to higher profits.

## Our business idea

To offer organisations a high-quality analysis tool, at a competitive price, to improve their results.
