The tool shows similarities and differences in how people behave, so that together we can make the most of everyone's strengths. Understanding each other is hard – understanding each other at work is harder still. PulsAnalys helps you understand yourself and others better.

PulsAnalys is based on DISC theory, which has its roots in the work of the American William M. Marston. [Read more about DISC](/en/what-is-disc/).

**With PulsAnalys you**

- improve understanding between managers and employees
- create a shared language inside the organisation
- get a simple, clear and fast tool.

**PulsAnalys measures**

- basic behaviour – the social self
- adaptation under pressure
- adaptation in the work environment.

## Step 1 – Basic DISC Training

To use the tool yourself, you take our [basic training](/en/basic-disc-training/). We put great weight on the feedback session and focus on it throughout the course. As the person giving feedback, your goal is to develop and lift people up. That builds understanding and good communication between managers and employees, and between colleagues. Clear communication is one of the keys to success.

We work mainly with people in HR, leaders, coaches, consultants and recruiters. The tool is used in recruitment, leadership development, group development and team building, personal development, performance reviews and coaching.

## Step 2 – Certification

Basic training includes ten profiles to practise on before certification. With them you practise giving feedback using our feedback structure and our ethical guidelines. Because the tool is about people, it is never possible to predict how someone will answer, so practising feedback matters. Validity is very high, but each person must decide for themselves how much of the profile fits.

Once certified, you can use all the tools in the system and are ready for a subscription.
