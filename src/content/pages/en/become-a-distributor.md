PulsAnalys is a communication tool that shows behavioural patterns based on DISC theory – a simple tool for understanding each other better at work.

In return, we expect you to run an active business and to have solid sales experience. We are looking for someone who builds good business relationships and has the drive and ambition to sell well. When you start, you need to have completed basic training, be a certified PulsAnalys user and have an active annual or monthly subscription.

We offer a unique product system in which customers build their own database. You decide your own approach and plan your own week. You are paid on commission.

## What we expect from you as a distributor

- You work independently and have your own car and an office with the equipment you need.
- You are outgoing and credible, with previous sales experience.
- You run an active business.
- When you start, you have completed basic training, are a certified PulsAnalys user and have an active annual or monthly subscription.

The need for our product is great, but customers have to learn that the solution exists – through good sales relationships and, above all, satisfied customers. Meeting customers' needs properly takes commitment and the patience to learn a new product.

## What we offer you

- You sell company subscriptions and our DISC training yourself.
- High commission on sales of courses and company subscriptions.
- A unique product system in which you build your own database.
- You decide your own approach and plan your own week.
