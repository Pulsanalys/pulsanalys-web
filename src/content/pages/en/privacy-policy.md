## Data controller

PulsAnalys AB is the data controller for the processing of personal data on this website. We make sure that you as a visitor

- know why and how we collect your personal data
- can choose to accept or decline the processing and storage of your personal data
- can update your details
- can be forgotten, so that the information we hold about you is deleted
- can receive all the personal data we hold about you
- can ask us to export your personal data, for example when changing supplier
- have your personal data kept securely. We do not pass it to third parties without telling you and getting your explicit consent to how and why.

## Personal data and cookies

Personal data is anything that can identify you: your name, address, personal identity number, e-mail address and so on. Cookies are small files sent to your computer through your browser when you visit a website. They collect information about how visitors use the website, often in order to offer tailored content.

Our cookies are there to improve your experience of the website and to collect traffic data about browsing, so that we can improve the website. Even where cookies are not legally personal data, we treat them as such.

## Why do we store personal data and cookies?

The aim of the website is to give our visitors the best possible experience. We value simplicity and an intuitive interface, and want you to understand the website and its functions quickly.

## How long do we keep cookies?

We keep cookies only as long as needed for their purpose, and for no more than three years after your last visit.

## If you complete a profile

When you complete a profile with PulsAnalys AB, some personal data is stored – normally your first and last name and a link to the e-mail address you provided. The purpose is to create a secure and reliable environment and as accurate an analysis as possible. We may need to contact you about your profile and related reports.

## If you have a company account

When you create an account with PulsAnalys AB, some personal data is stored – normally first and last name, company details such as the company registration number, and a link to the e-mail address you provided. The purpose is to create a secure and reliable administration environment and as accurate an analysis as possible. We also need to be able to contact you about your account and support. These details are never shown to anyone but you as a logged-in user. For business users we store personal data because each company account is linked to one or more users.

## Giving and withdrawing consent

You can prevent cookies from being stored, and delete stored cookies, in your browser. How you do this depends on the browser, so please see its help pages.

To create an account with PulsAnalys AB you need to become a member. To end your membership and have your whole account deleted, e-mail [support@pulsanalys.se](mailto:support@pulsanalys.se).

## Updating your details

Update your personal data in your user account (when logged in) or by e-mailing [support@pulsanalys.se](mailto:support@pulsanalys.se).

## Being forgotten

To have your details deleted from our systems and records, e-mail [support@pulsanalys.se](mailto:support@pulsanalys.se).

## Receiving or exporting your details

We do not pass your details to any other party. If you would like the details we hold about you sent to your e-mail, contact [support@pulsanalys.se](mailto:support@pulsanalys.se).

## Contact us

If you have any questions about how and why we handle personal data, contact us at [support@pulsanalys.se](mailto:support@pulsanalys.se).
